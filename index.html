AtibaExplainerVideos

Automated, fact-checked space-history YouTube Shorts for one YouTube channel.

What this application does

AtibaExplainerVideos is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the AtibaExplainerVideos YouTube channel. It is not a service offered to other people, and only the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel are connected to it.

Once a day it can:

  • select a space-history topic and collect public historical sources (for example Wikipedia and NASA documents);
  • write a short English script and check its historical claims against those sources;
  • create narration, original 2D animation, captions and sound, and render a vertical video (1080×1920);
  • upload the finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel as private.

Videos are never published automatically. A video only becomes public after the channel owner has reviewed and approved it.

Use of YouTube

The application uses the YouTube Data API through Google OAuth to upload videos to the owner's channel, to read the channel's recent uploads in order to avoid duplicate uploads, and to change a video's privacy status after the owner's approval. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
